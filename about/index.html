Built on Trust, Crafted with Care

Mission Decks & Design started with a simple idea: homeowners deserve an outdoor living contractor who treats their project like it matters. Not a number on a production schedule, not a template with the address changed — a genuine partnership where your space, your priorities, and your budget drive every decision.

We are based in Holly Springs, NC, and we serve the Triangle — Holly Springs, Apex, Cary, Fuquay-Varina, Sanford, and Raleigh. Every project is owner-led, which means you work directly with the person who designed your space and who will be on-site during construction.

From the first conversation to the final walkthrough, we keep things clear, honest, and straightforward. Detailed estimates with no hidden numbers. Realistic timelines that we actually meet. Communication that does not disappear once the contract is signed.
